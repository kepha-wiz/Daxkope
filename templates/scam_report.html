Scam Awareness & Reporting

Invest Responsibly · Protect Yourself

Scammers often impersonate legitimate platforms to steal money, credentials, or personal information. Cash Flow Future AI is a simulation platform — we will never ask you to send real money to personal numbers, share OTPs, or click suspicious links. If someone contacts you claiming to represent us and asks for payment, it is a scam. Report it below so we can warn others.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • ⚠
    Guaranteed returns
    No legitimate investment can promise fixed profits.
  • ⚠
    Requests for OTPs or passwords
    Never share these with anyone — ever.
  • ⚠
    Pressure to act immediately
    Scammers create false urgency to bypass your judgement.
  • ⚠
    Payments to personal numbers
    Legitimate businesses use verified merchant codes.
  • ⚠
    Unsolicited "investment opportunities"
    Be sceptical of cold calls, DMs, and WhatsApp messages.
